GoatedBuy buying guide

  1. Shortlist your GoatedBuy finds. Save the seller product link, item reference, size chart, and color option together. Check the exact variant before submitting it to your agent.
  2. Keep a comparison record. Note the listed item price, domestic delivery charge, seller stock status, and the date you checked each entry. This makes it easier to spot a changed listing.
  3. Review the warehouse inspection. Compare the order notes with the photos and measurements. If something differs, check the seller return window and the agent’s return process before approving shipment.
  4. Compare quotes for the packed parcel. Check actual and volumetric weight, tracking, and any optional insurance. Record the quote currency and review which costs are payable separately.

Fit, quality and shipping

Sneaker sizing and clothing fit

Compare sneaker insole length with a pair you already wear. For apparel, lay a similar garment flat and measure chest width, sleeve length, and shoulder width. For pants, compare waist and inseam. Check whether the garment measurements are in centimeters or inches.

How to read GoatedBuy QC photos

Compare sneaker outsole wear and stitching, jacket seams and zippers, or a cap’s brim and rear closure. For jewelry, look for finish marks and confirm that the clasp closes. Ask for a close-up or a ruler photo when the angle makes a detail hard to judge.

Building a GoatedBuy shipping estimate

Start with item cost, domestic freight, any agent fees, and international shipping. Compare parcel consolidation and separate parcels using packed dimensions. For deliveries to the US, UK, or EU, confirm destination coverage and check whether the quote includes applicable import charges.

Frequently asked questions

What is a GoatedBuy spreadsheet?

It is a curated directory of product references or seller links that helps shoppers find items to review before ordering through a shopping agent.

How do I check if a product listing is still active?

Open the seller listing, choose the exact size and color, and confirm that the variant is available before submitting a purchase request.

What should I check in QC photos?

Check the selected size and color, visible defects, and measurements where available. Request a clearer photo if an important detail is missing.

How can I estimate international shipping?

Use your destination, estimated packed weight and dimensions, and a current shipping quote. Check whether the carrier uses volumetric weight and which fees or taxes the quote includes.
